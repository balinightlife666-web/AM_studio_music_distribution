Privacy Policy

How AM STUDIO handles platform and release data.

TermsPrivacyDistribution AgreementCopyright & Takedown

1. Data we process

The platform may process account identifiers, session information, release metadata, artist and contributor information, audio masters, artwork, technical file information, review decisions, audit events, support communications, and royalty or payout information when those features are enabled.

2. Why data is used

Data is used to operate the service, verify uploads, review releases, maintain a catalog, secure the platform, investigate errors or rights complaints, support users, produce accounting records, and deliver releases through authorized providers when enabled.

3. Storage

Platform metadata is stored in Cloudflare-hosted infrastructure. Media storage may use Cloudflare object storage or a controlled compatibility store while migration is in progress. Access to administrative media is protected by platform authorization.

4. Third parties

When distribution, payment, KYC, analytics, or support integrations are enabled, necessary data may be sent to the corresponding service providers. AM STUDIO does not treat an inactive integration as if data had been delivered to that provider.

5. Retention and deletion

Records may be retained where reasonably required for catalog integrity, audit, accounting, fraud prevention, rights disputes, legal obligations, or provider reconciliation. Requests concerning personal data can be sent to the contact address below.

6. Security

Reasonable technical controls are used, including authenticated administrative access, checksums, server-authoritative records, and audit logging. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
