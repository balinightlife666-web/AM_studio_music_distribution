AM STUDIO provides tools to prepare releases, upload music and artwork, run technical checks, submit releases for internal review, maintain audit records, and display royalty information when available.

2. Current delivery status

The platform may operate in a sandbox or pre-provider state. An internal status such as APPROVED does not by itself mean that a release has been delivered to, accepted by, or published on any DSP. Users are responsible for keeping credentials and access devices secure. Abuse, automated attacks, credential sharing that creates security risk, or attempts to bypass platform controls may result in access restriction.
