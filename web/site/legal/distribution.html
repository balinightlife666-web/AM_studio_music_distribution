Operational distribution terms. Provider delivery remains disabled until an authorized connection is activated.

1. Authorization

By submitting a release, the rights holder authorizes PT NADMO STUDIO INDONESIA to process the supplied assets and metadata for review and, only when a contracted distribution provider is enabled, to transmit the approved release to selected DSPs.

2. Rights grant

The user grants the limited rights reasonably necessary to host, validate, format, transmit, administer, report on, and where requested remove the submitted release. Ownership of the underlying music and artwork is not transferred by this operational grant.

3. User warranties

The user represents that all required master, composition, performer, artwork, name, likeness, sample, and other permissions have been obtained and that the metadata supplied is accurate.

4. Identifiers

ISRC, UPC/EAN/GTIN and catalog identifiers must be legitimate. The platform does not fabricate official identifiers. Where an authorized provider later offers identifier assignment, that assignment must be recorded as provider-issued or otherwise lawfully assigned.

5. DSP delivery

Internal approval is separate from external delivery. A release is considered sent only when the provider adapter records a successful delivery transaction. A release is considered live only when reliable store/provider evidence confirms publication.

6. Royalties

Royalty balances become authoritative only from imported or reconciled provider statements and corresponding ledger entries. Sandbox balances, empty ledgers, or internal approval do not create a payment obligation.

7. Takedowns and corrections

Users may request a correction or takedown. Third-party rights complaints may result in a hold or takedown while ownership is reviewed. Provider and DSP processing times remain outside AM STUDIO's direct control.

8. Commercial activation

Fees, commissions, revenue share, payment timing, tax withholding, payout threshold and other commercial conditions must be accepted separately before commercial distribution is activated.
