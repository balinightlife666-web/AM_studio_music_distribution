Copyright & Takedown Policy

Rights complaints, holds, corrections and takedown requests.

TermsPrivacyDistribution AgreementCopyright & Takedown

1. Rights-first policy

Only content for which the submitting party owns or controls the required rights may be submitted. Unauthorized samples, artwork, recordings, compositions, names or other protected materials are not permitted.

2. Reporting a claim

A rights holder may send a notice to nadmostudio@gmail.com identifying the work, disputed release, basis of ownership, requested action, and reliable contact information. Supporting evidence may be requested.

3. Platform response

AM STUDIO may place a release in RIGHTS_HOLD, request clarification, preserve relevant audit records, prevent new delivery attempts, and initiate provider/DSP takedown procedures when those connections exist.

4. Counter-information

The submitting user may provide evidence of ownership, license, consent, or other authorization. AM STUDIO may keep a release on hold while conflicting claims are reviewed.

5. Repeat or fraudulent submissions

Repeated infringement, forged documents, intentionally false metadata, or attempts to evade rights controls may result in account restriction or termination.

6. No false delivery claims

A takedown is not represented as completed until the relevant provider or DSP state confirms the action where external delivery exists.
